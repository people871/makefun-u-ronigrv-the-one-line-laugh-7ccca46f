## The line

> My smartwatch congratulated me on 10,000 steps, then admitted 9,400 of them were just me pacing while the microwave finished.

## Why this line

The judge weighs a real surprise at the end, an everyday truth people recognise, and economy of words, in that order. The surprise is the turn in the second half: the watch's proud congratulation collapses into an admission that nearly all the steps were pacing in front of the microwave. The everyday truth is the step counter itself — anyone who owns a tracker has watched it celebrate movement that was not exercise at all. Nothing is wasted: every word either sets up the praise or delivers the deflation, and the punchline arrives in the last few words, where the judge's brief wants it.
